Privacy

Chore Story is a free tool for families. It collects as little as possible and never sells or shares your data.

What we store

  • The parent's email address, used only to send sign-in codes.
  • A family name, time zone and a scrambled (hashed) parent PIN.
  • Each kid's first name and color, allowance amounts, job board, earnings history, reading log (book titles and minutes) and screen-time sessions.

We don't collect kids' last names, birthdays, photos, locations, contact details or anything else. Kids never create accounts or enter an email; the parent's account holds everything.

Who can see it

Only the parents signed in to your family. Data is stored with Supabase and protected by database rules that keep each family's records separate. There are no ads, trackers or analytics.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Parent → Settings → Delete account and all data. It permanently removes your family, every kid profile and all history right away.

Donations

Donation links go to Venmo or Cash App. Chore Story never sees your payment details.
