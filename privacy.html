Privacy

The short version

Paper Pig is a free tool for families. It keeps as little as possible, never sells or shares it, and has no ads, trackers or analytics.

What we store

The parent's email address, used only to send sign-in codes. A family name, time zone and a scrambled (hashed) parent PIN. For each kid: a first name and color, allowance amount, job board, jobs and bonuses with their explanations, payments and purchases with their explanations, jar entries with their reasons (only if you turn the jar on), a saving goal if one is set, reading sessions (book titles and time) and screen-time sessions.

What we don't collect

No last names, birthdays, photos, locations, school names, contact details or anything else about kids. Kids never make an account or enter an email. Everything sits under the parent's account.

Who can see it

Only the parents signed in to your family. Data is stored with Supabase and protected by database rules that keep each family's records separate from every other family's.

Feedback messages

If you send a bug report, idea or message, it's delivered to the person who runs this site, along with the email you typed (optional) and which screen you were on.

Donations

The Venmo, Cash App and PayPal links open those services. This site never sees your payment details.

Deleting your data

Parent → Settings → Delete account and all data permanently removes your family, every kid profile and all history right away.

Questions

Use Contact on the sign-in screen, or Settings → Contact & feedback.
